## 6. 🚀 **How to Use Voice Feature**

### Step-by-Step:
1. **Start an interview**
2. **See the question** from AI
3. **Click the microphone icon** (bottom-right)
4. **Grant microphone permission** (browser popup)
5. **Start speaking** - text appears automatically
6. **Click red stop button** when done
7. **Edit if needed** - you can still type
8. **Click "Submit Answer"**

### Tips for Best Results:
- **Speak clearly** and at normal pace
- **Pause between sentences** for accuracy
- **Works best in quiet environment**
- **Chrome/Edge** recommended for best support
- **Can switch between voice and typing** anytime
